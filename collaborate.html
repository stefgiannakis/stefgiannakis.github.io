For collaborators and consortia

The useful question is not “what are my interests?” but “what scientific responsibility can I credibly own?”

I can lead

  • Environmental AMR / ARB / ARG experimental work
  • Mechanistic treatment and disinfection studies
  • Real-water validation and robustness analysis
  • Comparative technology benchmarking and operating windows
  • Harmonized experimental protocols and KPI frameworks
  • Scientific integration of multi-partner experimental work

I contribute with specialist partners

  • Shotgun metagenomics and advanced bioinformatics
  • Mobilome reconstruction and advanced HGT analysis
  • Core AI / machine-learning development
  • Atmospheric aerosol physics
  • Advanced epidemiological / transmission modelling
  • Full LCA/LCC and advanced socioeconomic modelling

Capability matrix

If your project needs…

Environmental AMR

ARB/ARG endpoints, targeted qPCR, microbial state, regrowth, biofilms and environmental fate.

Novel treatment technology

Mechanistic validation, benchmark comparison, realistic matrices and operating windows.

Materials / catalysts

Microbiological, mechanistic and safety evidence gates before process progression.

Water reuse

Chemical + microbial treatment performance under realistic reuse conditions.

Digital water

Experimentally meaningful variables, structured datasets, validation scenarios and interpretation.

Cross-partner scientific WP

Common methods, QA/QC logic, KPI framework and integrated experimental interpretation.

For collaborators and consortia

I prefer explicit scientific ownership: lead where the evidence is strong, transfer methods where justified, and rely on specialist partners where another discipline should own the work.

I can lead

  • Mechanistic treatment and disinfection studies
  • Environmental AMR / ARB / ARG experimental work
  • Chemical–microbial validation under realistic matrices
  • Comparative technology benchmarking and operating windows
  • Harmonized experimental protocols and WP-level KPIs
  • Scientific integration of multi-partner experimental work

I contribute with specialist partners

  • Shotgun metagenomics and advanced bioinformatics
  • Mobilome reconstruction and advanced HGT studies
  • Transmission modelling and epidemiological integration
  • Core AI / machine-learning model development
  • Atmospheric aerosol physics
  • Advanced socioeconomic modelling and full LCA/LCC ownership

Where I am especially useful

Typical project roles

  • Environmental AMR and water-risk work packages — integrating treatment, molecular endpoints and environmental relevance.
  • Treatment validation work packages — benchmarking technologies, establishing operating windows and robustness criteria.
  • Materials and technology assessment — evidence-based go/no-go decisions before process scaling.
  • Digital-water consortia — generating and interpreting the environmental-process layer that feeds predictive tools.
  • Reuse and implementation projects — connecting microbiological and chemical performance to practical validation logic.

How to start

Useful first message

If you want to explore collaboration, the most useful first message contains:

  • the call text or topic title,
  • the specific scientific problem you need solved,
  • the expected project role (PI, WP leader, task leader or partner),
  • any known consortium gaps.

About

Environmental and civil engineer working across chemical, photochemical and microbiological mechanisms in water systems.

Biography

From solar disinfection mechanisms to connected environmental pathways

I am Profesor Titular de Universidad (i3) at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), ETSI de Caminos, Canales y Puertos, Department of Civil Engineering: Hydraulics, Energy and Environment, and Deputy Director of CIVILis.

My research combines environmental chemistry, photochemistry, microbiology and engineering validation. The recurring question is how mechanistic knowledge can be translated into treatment decisions that remain meaningful under realistic environmental conditions.

Before my current appointment, I was a Ramón y Cajal Fellow at UPM and previously worked at EPFL in Switzerland. My research trajectory also includes international stays and collaborations in Italy, the United States and France.

Experimental capabilities

Laboratory capacity is presented in terms of what it enables scientifically, not as a catalogue of equipment.

Molecular

Targeted molecular environmental microbiology

Two qPCR instruments, automated DNA extraction at approximately 96 samples/hour, 96-well nucleic-acid quantification and high-throughput targeted molecular workflows.

  • ARG/MGE targets
  • pre/post treatment
  • large campaigns

Cellular

Microbiology & phenotype

Culture-based microbiology, resistant strains, regrowth and biofilm experiments, supported by a multimode microplate reader for fluorescence, bioluminescence and chemiluminescence assays.

  • growth
  • viability
  • biofilms
  • stress response

Process

Photochemistry & treatment

Controlled solar/UV treatment, advanced oxidation/reduction experiments and mechanistic assays linking reactive chemistry to microbial and contaminant outcomes.

  • AOP/AORP
  • real matrices
  • kinetics

What this enables

Integrated experimental validation

  • Targeted AMR monitoring: high-throughput ARG/MGE and microbial marker campaigns.
  • Phenotype–genotype coupling: connect molecular endpoints to culturability, regrowth and stress response.
  • Treatment response: compare microbial and chemical outcomes across operating conditions and matrices.
  • Model-ready evidence: structured process datasets with defined metadata, controls and validation scenarios.
  • Technology gates: determine whether materials or treatment candidates merit progression.

Boundary

Targeted molecular analytics ≠ omics lab

The platform supports targeted qPCR-based environmental molecular microbiology. Untargeted shotgun metagenomics, advanced mobilomics and specialist bioinformatics remain collaboration domains.

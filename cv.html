Professional overview

Stefanos Giannakis is Profesor Titular de Universidad (i3) at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), ETSI de Caminos, Canales y Puertos, Department of Civil Engineering: Hydraulics, Energy and Environment, and Deputy Director of the UPM Centre for R&D&I in Civil, Intelligent and Sustainable Infrastructures (CIVILis).

His trajectory combines civil/environmental engineering with environmental chemistry, photochemistry, treatment science and microbiology, including previous work at UPM and EPFL, with additional international research experience.

He holds a jointly supervised PhD in Environmental Engineering from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki and Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya.

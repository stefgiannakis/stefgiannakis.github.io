Research profile

Research logic

Why the “connected pathway” view matters

Many environmental problems are misread when treatment is treated as an isolated event. I am interested in the chain linking process mechanism, microbial state, prior exposure, environmental fate and eventual risk or implementation consequence.

  1. 01MechanismChemical or photochemical processes controlling inactivation or transformation
  2. 02StateViability, sublethal stress, resistance, phenotype and genotype
  3. 03HistoryBiological or treatment memory affecting later performance
  4. 04FatePersistence, attenuation, transformation and mobility in the environment
  5. 05InterfaceWater to crops, biofilms, receiving waters and other connected compartments
  6. 06ImplementationDecision criteria, reproducibility, scale relevance and KPIs

Methods

Methodological toolbox

  • Experimental design: mechanism-oriented controls, benchmarking, operating envelopes, realistic matrices.
  • Microbiology: culture-based assays, resistant strains, biofilms, regrowth and stress-response experiments.
  • Molecular analytics: targeted qPCR workflows, extraction/QC pipelines, phenotype–genotype integration.
  • Photochemistry & treatment: light-assisted processes, AOP/AORP systems, contaminant and disinfection studies.
  • Integration: harmonized datasets and experimental evidence for project-wide decision making.

Important boundaries

What I do not overclaim

My profile is strongest in environmental engineering, microbiology and mechanism-oriented treatment science. Some topics are best owned by specialist partners.

  • Shotgun metagenomics and advanced bioinformatics
  • Mobilome reconstruction and advanced HGT analysis
  • Core machine-learning model development
  • Atmospheric aerosol physics
  • Advanced socioeconomic modelling or full LCA/LCC ownership

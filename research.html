Research logic

Follow the pathway, not just the reactor

My recent work increasingly treats environmental performance as a connected pathway: process mechanism → biological state → treatment history → environmental fate → exposure interface → implementation.

  1. 01MechanismWhat chemistry actually drives change?
  2. 02Biological stateWhat survives, adapts or rebounds?
  3. 03HistoryDoes upstream stress reshape downstream behavior?
  4. 04FateWhat persists after release?
  5. 05InterfaceWhere does exposure occur?
  6. 06ImplementationWhat evidence supports deployment?

Claim boundary

Specialist partners still matter

The in-house profile supports targeted molecular AMR analytics and mechanistic environmental microbiology. Shotgun metagenomics, mobilome reconstruction, advanced HGT quantification, core ML development and advanced epidemiology remain specialist-partner domains.

Portfolio evolution

How the research has moved over time

The 24-project audit shows a clear progression rather than a random collection of proposal topics.

2019–2021

Treatment, reuse & circularity

Solar AOPs, industrial/urban water reuse, contaminant removal, circular treatment and reactor/process development.

2022–2024

AMR becomes a treatment-quality variable

ARB/ARGs, pathogens and emerging contaminants become integrated treatment endpoints alongside digital-water and reuse questions.

2023–2026

Mechanisms and biological state

Redox biology, treatment-induced stress, regrowth, biofilms and persistent/mobile contaminants move to the center of the experimental logic.

2025–2026

Connected environmental pathways

The research expands beyond the reactor toward treatment history, environmental fate, agrifood interfaces, water-to-air transfer, implementation and evidence-gated technology development.

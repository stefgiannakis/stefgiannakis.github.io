Mechanism → application

Environmental engineering for safer, more resilient water systems.

I work at the interface of environmental chemistry, photochemistry and microbiology to understand how treatment and environmental conditions control contaminants, pathogens and antimicrobial resistance — and to translate that mechanistic knowledge into practical treatment, validation and risk-reduction strategies.

Problems I work on

The common thread is mechanism-to-application environmental engineering: understand what controls performance, then test whether it survives contact with realistic matrices and biological complexity.

01

Environmental AMR & microbial fate

ARB/ARGs, microbial stress, biofilms, regrowth and the difference between molecular signal and biologically relevant risk.
